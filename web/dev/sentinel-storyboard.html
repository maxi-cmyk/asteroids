Sentinel: the armoured eye

A mock-up for approval (nextSteps.md P4.1). The shell, eye and brows are draft art (tools/make-p4-drafts.py, sheet draft-p4-sentinel); the plates are the game's own. Its core stays: shoot the eye while it is open. What changes: the open eye reads at a glance, it watches you, it slides on a rail, and a new plate throw makes being brave pay.
