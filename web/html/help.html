How to Play

Clear each wave, take down the bosses, and keep your lives. In co-op, P1 is the cyan diamond and P2 the amber square: three lives each, no revives.

Your controls

Flight

Move, aim, survive each wave. One shot per press.

Loadouts & secondary fire

Choose a ship, primary weapon and secondary weapon independently in the Hangar. The starter kit is free; clearing Levels 2–6 unlocks their kits. Primary fire accepts presses at the weapon’s rate. Hold secondary to charge, release early to cancel; charge and cooldown depend on the ability. Beam charges for 1.5 s and cools for 5 s.

Shield & precision

Tap Shield to arm a stored charge. Hold Precision to move slowly.

Bosses

  • Weak points each boss can only be hurt in one place, often only for a few seconds: watch for the part that opens, glows or loses its shield.
  • Warnings dotted lines, rings and glows show where an attack will land before it does; they blink just before it fires.
  • Hits a pink burst means your shot hurt; a gold spark means it was stopped.

Campaign

Play → Campaign: six levels, one for each region. Each is five waves, a mini-boss on wave 3 and the region’s boss on wave 5. Clear a level to open the next; you get three lives per level, and losing them all means a retry, never the end of the act.

Stars

Three per level: clear it, finish with at least the 3 lives you started with (extra lives offset losses), and finish under the time target. Your best stars, time and score are kept on Records → Campaign. In co-op each pilot earns their own stars.

Endless

Finish all six levels to open Endless: today’s run with no end, and the top-five tables. Practice and the demo never change your campaign progress.

Practice

Play → Practice: pick a drill. In flight, T (or View / Select) opens the tools: start any wave or fight any boss. Multishot and 2× fire toggles sit on the bottom bar.

Pickups

  • Multishot three-way volley for six seconds.
  • Shield stores one charge that blocks a hit.
  • 2× fire doubles your shots for six seconds.
  • Health a defeated boss leaves one: +1 life, up to five. Mini-bosses drop one half the time.

Finish or quit your run before Practice or a demo flight.
